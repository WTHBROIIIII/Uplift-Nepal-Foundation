What We Do

Our Focus Areas

We work across four pillars to create lasting, meaningful change in communities across Nepal.

📚

Education

Empowering communities through quality education, skills development, and access to learning opportunities for all.

🏥

Health

Providing healthcare resources, guidance, and support to ensure every person has access to essential health services.

🌍

Environment

Protecting Nepal's precious natural heritage through sustainable initiatives and community-led environmental action.

🤝

Youth & Community

Nurturing the next generation of leaders and strengthening communities through inclusion and opportunity.

Rooted in Nepal. Driven by Purpose.

Every smile tells a story. Every act of kindness creates an impact. At Uplift Nepal Foundation, we believe that real change begins with compassion, action, and community.

Our mission is to empower communities through education, healthcare, environmental initiatives, disaster relief, and youth development across Nepal. Together, we can create opportunities, inspire hope, and build a brighter future for generations to come.
